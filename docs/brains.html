Part 5 · Any brain

The cage is the product.
The brain is swappable.

Apollo starts with Claude because it's the best at finishing real projects today. But the walls, the phone buzz, the kill switch and the logbook don't care which AI is inside. Nobody has to be a Claude subscriber to use it.
