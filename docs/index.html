Why the name

Hermes stole the cattle.
Apollo caught him.

In the myth, Hermes was a few hours old when he snuck out, stole Apollo's sacred cattle, and walked them backwards so the tracks pointed the wrong way. Apollo tracked him down anyway, dragged him in front of Zeus, and made him confess.

We were running an AI agent called Hermes. It did Hermes things. So we built the god who checks the tracks.
